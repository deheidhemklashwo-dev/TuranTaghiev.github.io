Approach

I work within a neorealist frame, but treat theory as a tool for strategy rather than an end in itself. The question is always practical: how does a given actor convert capability into leverage, and what does that reveal about the order being built?

My method is qualitative and case-driven — close reading of policy, regulation, and corporate behaviour, paired with structured data where it sharpens the argument. I write in English and Turkish, with a regional focus on East Asia and the wider Indo-Pacific.
