Approach

I work within a neorealist frame, but treat theory as a tool for strategy rather than an end in itself. The question is always practical: how does a given actor convert capability into leverage, and what does that reveal about the order being built?

My approach is qualitative and built around cases. I read policy, regulation and the way companies behave closely, and bring in data when it makes the argument clearer. I write in English and Turkish, and most of my work centres on East Asia and the wider Indo-Pacific.

I had the opportunity to lead Turan as part of our extended team over the past few years. I first worked with him when he joined us as a Discord Moderator in October 2023. Over time, he consistently demonstrated the professionalism, reliability, and willingness to learn that led to his transition into an outsourced Technical Support role in September 2024.

As a moderator, Turan took ownership of many aspects of managing our Turkish Discord community. He independently reviewed user reports, investigated rule violations, enforced our community guidelines, and handled escalations across both our Turkish- and English-language channels. He also worked closely with our helper team by reviewing their ban requests, making moderation decisions, and ensuring actions were applied consistently and fairly. Beyond day-to-day moderation, he regularly gathered player feedback on new game updates and collaborated effectively with fellow moderators and team leads to address emerging community concerns.

One area where Turan consistently stood out was his commitment to continuous improvement. He regularly asked for feedback, took it seriously, and put in the effort to improve his performance. As a team lead, I always appreciated that mindset because it reflected a genuine desire to grow and deliver better results.

In his Technical Support role, Turan handled customer inquiries, bug reports, and other support cases with professionalism and care. He followed established procedures consistently, exercised good judgment, and approached his work with a strong sense of responsibility. He also took initiative beyond his core responsibilities by preparing governance and community health reports for our Brazilian team, highlighting moderation trends, recurring issues, and key metrics that helped regional community managers identify problems early and make informed decisions.

Throughout the time we worked together, I found Turan to be dependable, receptive to coaching, and someone I could trust to carry out his responsibilities to a high standard. It has been rewarding to see his growth from a community moderator into a trusted member of our Technical Support team. I appreciate Turan's contributions and would confidently recommend him to any organization looking for a dedicated, coachable, and reliable professional. I have no doubt he will continue to grow and make a meaningful impact wherever he goes, and I wish him every success in the next chapter of his career.
